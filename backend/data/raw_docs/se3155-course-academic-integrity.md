# Academic integrity requirements

Course: Software Engineering 3155
Term: Fall 2026
Scope: Course-wide
Content type: academic_integrity_policy
Confidence: verified
Keywords: own work, citation, plagiarism

Academic integrity requirements: 1) Submitted work must be the student's own. 2) External references used in reports must be cited. 3) Duplicate or plagiarized work receives no credit and may lead to disciplinary action under the university academic-integrity code.
