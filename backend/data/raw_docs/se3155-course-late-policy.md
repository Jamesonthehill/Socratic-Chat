# Assignment and quiz late policy

Course: Software Engineering 3155
Term: Fall 2026
Scope: Course-wide
Content type: late_policy
Confidence: verified
Keywords: late penalty, one week, quiz

Assignment and quiz late policy: 1) The stated assignment late penalty is 5% per day. 2) Assignments may be submitted up to one week late with a maximum stated penalty of 30%; after that point the stated result is zero credit. 3) Quizzes do not allow late submission. 4) Potential exceptions are handled case by case and require timely communication and appropriate justification through official course channels.
